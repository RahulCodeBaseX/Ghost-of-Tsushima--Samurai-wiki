Jin Sakai Relationships

Important people in Jin Sakai's life.

Lord Shimura

Lord Shimura is Jin's uncle and the Jito of Tsushima. He raised Jin after the death of Jin's father and trained him in the samurai way.

Yuna

Yuna is a skilled thief who becomes one of Jin's closest allies during the Mongol invasion. She helps Jin survive and supports his transformation into the Ghost.

Lady Masako

Lady Masako Adachi is a samurai warrior who joins Jin while seeking justice for the destruction of her family and clan.

Sensei Ishikawa

Ishikawa is an experienced archer and former teacher. Jin works with him while investigating the disappearance of Ishikawa's former student.

Taka

Taka is a talented blacksmith and Yuna's brother. He helps Jin by creating and improving equipment.
